# Open company lists

`companies_open_lists.csv` is built from two public lists of companies and their job boards:

- [7bshiup/ats-career-boards](https://github.com/7bshiup/ats-career-boards), CC BY 4.0
- [datakit457/ats-company-slugs](https://github.com/datakit457/ats-company-slugs), CC0

SmartRecruiters and Personio entries are left out (SmartRecruiters' API host closes itself to bots
in robots.txt; Personio has no adapter yet). `companies.csv` is the curated list and wins when both
name the same board.
